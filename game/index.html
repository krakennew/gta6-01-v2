SOLMAR KEYS

An open-world crime game on the island of Solmar

You are a driver for hire with nothing but a borrowed car and a phone full of Rico Vega's jobs. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar PD, and run three jobs — a pickup, a getaway, and the big score — to own the island.

WASD / Arrows — walk or drive
E — get in / out of a car (stealing one brings the police)
F / Click — punch
Shift — run  ·  P — pause  ·  M — mute

Solmar Keys is an original game. Its city, characters, vehicles, names and story are its own — nothing from any commercial game. Built for make.fun.
